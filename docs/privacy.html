Lossless Radio – Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

Lossless Radio ("the app") is an internet radio player developed by DeveshB-1.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting. The developer receives no data from the app.

Network connections

  • Station directory – to list and search stations, the app queries radio-browser.info, a community-run directory. When you play a directory station, the app tells the directory which station was played (no personal data), which feeds its popularity ranking.
  • Streams and logos – audio and station logos are fetched directly from each station's own servers.

As with any internet request, these servers receive your IP address and basic request information, under their own privacy policies.

Data stored on your device

Your favourites, recently played stations, stations you add by URL and the bit-perfect setting are stored on your device only, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Optional tips

Tips are processed entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. The developer never sees your payment details. Tips unlock nothing.

Permissions

  • Internet – to stream radio and load the station directory.
  • Foreground service / wake lock – to keep playing with the screen off.
  • Modify audio settings – to enable bit-perfect USB output when you turn it on.
  • Google Play billing – to process optional tips.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/DeveshB-1/lossless-radio.
